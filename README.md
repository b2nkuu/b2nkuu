## 🎯 The Developer

> *"From junior .NET developer in 2012 to co-founding my own venture in 2025 — every line of code has been a step toward building something meaningful."*

I'm a **Software Developer** with over a decade of hands-on experience crafting digital solutions across the full spectrum of modern development. My journey began in the enterprise .NET world, evolved through native mobile development (Java, Swift, Xamarin), and crystallized into a deep specialization in **Flutter** and **Golang** ecosystems.

Currently, I'm wearing multiple hats: **Software Engineer Specialist** at [Bitkub Blockchain Technology](https://www.bitkubblockchain.com), where I shape the future of blockchain interfaces, and **Co-Founder** at [P'nB Solutions](https://pnb.solutions), where we're building the next generation of developer tools and automation.

My philosophy blends Japanese craftsmanship principles ([Ikigai](https://github.com/b2nkuu/spirit), [Kaizen](https://github.com/b2nkuu/spirit), [Shokunin](https://github.com/b2nkuu/spirit)) with cutting-edge tech — because great software is as much about mindset as it is about code.
